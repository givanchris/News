From a Simulation to a Decision

The cone widens with the square root of time — the longer the horizon, the wider the dispersion of plausible outcomes. The median path compounds to ≈ 1.46× over five years, but the model's value is the downside tail, not the headline.

Even a portfolio with an attractive median can carry an uncomfortable p5 outcome. Quantifying that — and stress-testing how rebalancing changes it — is the difference between a forecast and a risk decision.
