Methodology

How Each Model Works

Every company tab follows the same four-step structure, so the models can be compared side by side.

01 · Operating model
Guidance-anchored forecast

Five years of revenue, gross and operating margin, cash taxes, D&A, capex and working capital. Year one is tied to reported results and management guidance; later years carry the scenario.

02 · Cash flow
Unlevered FCF + fade

NOPAT + D&A − capex − ΔNWC for the explicit years, then a second five-year stage where FCF growth steps down toward the terminal rate.

03 · Discount rate
Company-specific WACC

CAPM cost of equity from the 10-year Treasury (4.9%), Damodaran's implied equity risk premium (4.18%) and a company beta, blended with an after-tax cost of debt.

04 · Valuation
Value per share

Gordon-growth terminal value, discounted with the forecast cash flows to enterprise value, plus net cash, divided by diluted shares. Compared with the market price for implied upside.

Why bull cases

Each tab asks the same question: if the optimistic AI-demand story plays out, what is the business worth today? Where even that scenario comes out below the current price, the market is already pricing in more than the bull case.

How it was built

An AI-assisted build. Every forecast input is tied to a cited source on the workbook's Sources tab (10-Ks, 10-Qs, earnings releases and investor-day materials), so each assumption can be traced and changed.
